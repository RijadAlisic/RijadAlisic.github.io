Rijad Alisic

Researcher in Control of Dynamic Systems.

I make automatic control systems compatible with: cybersecurity adversarial learning environments privacy cybersecurity

I'm a Knut and Alice Wallenberg Research Fellow at MIT's Laboratory for Information and Decision Systems. My research is in automatic control: designing feedback loops for dynamic systems and studying how a system's data reveals its vulnerability to cyber attacks.

Outside the lab, I'm chasing local-legend status next to various waterways, reading and writing, and doing projects that make automatic control more publicly accessible beyond academia and industry.
